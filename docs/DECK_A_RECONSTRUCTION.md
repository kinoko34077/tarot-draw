## User print feedback — variable glyph heights and snake orientation (2026-10-12 / #93)
The supplied scan uses larger initial capitals in each word and smaller remaining small-cap letters, not one uniform all-uppercase font height. The three title lines retain reference widths and baselines while the generator now emits separate tspan font sizes for each word's initial (The/Original, Rider/Waite, Tarot/Deck). The actual difference is glyph height, not merely spacing.

The selected AnonMoos public-domain Ouroboros-simple SVG path was correctly sourced in #91 but its delivered image was upside-down. #93 rotates the group 180° relative to #91 by removing the extra rotate(180) (now rotate(0)) outside the native SVG's Y-inverted path. The path, frame, colour and license are unchanged; validate using rendered print proof.

## #97 letterpress title final font trial — 2026-10-12
The original physical image was reviewed as a reference only (not copied). Four comparable candidate font profiles were rendered as independent previews: EB Garamond Bold, Liberation Serif Bold, Noto Serif Display Bold, and C059 Bold. The selected choice is **Liberation Serif Bold** (fallback `Times New Roman`), prioritizing the reference's relatively heavy period serif and regular character proportions, without EB's stylized thick/thin extremes or Noto Display's tall-condensed contrast. Rendered candidate comparison belongs to #97's evidence and does not add proprietary font files.

The former generator combined all words on each title line under a single `textLength` with glyph height-changing `tspan` plus `dx`. This was fragile under font substitution and produced glyph collisions and a displaced title even in the radio settings card preview. The revised generator positions **each word in a separate x coordinate and independently bounded width** (The/Original =178+367 within587; Rider/Waite =263+261 within566; Tarot/Deck =252+265 within557). Word gaps are computed from remaining width (42,42,40 SVG units). Each word internally uses large first capital and smaller following capitals, so independent glyph compression cannot overflow into adjacent words. Baselines and overall size remain reference-like. Existing PD Ouroboros and rest of card unchanged.
